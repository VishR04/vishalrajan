SOLIDWORKS and 3-D Printing

I designed a hand cranked Ferris wheel mechanism in SOLIDWORKS using eight unique components and a 12–24–48 tooth gear train that produced a 4:1 speed reduction. I applied parametric modeling, assembly mates, motion constraints, and design for manufacturing principles to complete this project.

SolidWorks Mechanical Assembly Design Design for Manufacturing(DFM) Manufacturing Process Selection Mechanical Design Optimization

Mechanical Drawing and GD&T Analysis

The purpose of this project was to convert the Ferris Wheel assembly from the SOLIDWORKS and 3-D project into a production ready drawing package and specify functional tolerances for its rotating interfaces. Using SOLIDWORKS, I created an exploded assembly drawing, and fully dimensioned drawings for the wheel, axle, and support. I applied GD&T tolerancing and selected an ISO H8/f7 clearance fit, producing a 0.00050–0.00200 in clearance range for reliable rotation and interchangeability.

Geomtric Dimensioning and Tolerancing(GD&T) Engineering Drawings Limits and Fits Analysis Design For Manufacturing(DFM) Bills Of Materials Development(BOM)

Topology Optimization

The purpose of this project was to redesign a 6061-aluminum structural component subjected to a 50 lbf distributed load. I was tasked with minimizing its mass while maintaining a maximum displacement below 1 mil, comparing multiple design iterations, and validating the final results through mesh convergence and manual analysis. Using SOLIDWORKS Simulation, I completed five design iterations and reduced the component’s mass by 24.77% while maintaining a 2.85 factor of safety. I validated the FEA results through a nine point mesh convergence study and manual nodal analysis. I also applied design for manufacturing principles by preserving critical interfaces and load paths, maintaining practical feature geometry, and selecting a lightweight, machinable material.

Finite Element Analysis(FEA) SolidWorks Simulation Design Optimization Mesh Convergence Engineering Trade-Off Analysis
